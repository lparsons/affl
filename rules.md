## Conduct and Policies

### Activity Requirements
- Set lineups each week
- Respond to trade offers within 48 hours
- Participate in league discussions

### Collusion
- Collusion is strictly prohibited
- Suspected collusion will be reviewed by commissioner
- Penalties may include transaction reversal or removal from league

### Tanking
- All managers must field competitive lineups
- Obvious tanking will result in commissioner intervention

## Commissioner Powers

The commissioner has the authority to:
- Reverse transactions in cases of collusion
- Force lineup changes in cases of abandonment
- Resolve disputes and interpret rules
- Make emergency rule changes with league approval

- Major changes should be voted on before the season
