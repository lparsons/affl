# About The Art of Fantasy Football League (AFFL)

## Our Mission

The Art of Fantasy Football League (AFFL) was founded on the principle that fantasy football is more than just a game - it's an art form that combines statistical analysis, strategic thinking, and a deep understanding of the NFL.

## League Philosophy

We believe that fantasy football success comes from:

- **Strategic Planning** - Long-term thinking and roster construction
- **Statistical Analysis** - Using data to make informed decisions
- **Community Engagement** - Building relationships and friendly competition
- **Continuous Learning** - Adapting strategies and improving each season
