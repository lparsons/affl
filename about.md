# About The Art of Fantasy Football League (AFFL)

## Our Mission

The Art of Fantasy Football League (AFFL) was founded on the principle that fantasy football is more than just a game — it is an art form combining statistical analysis, strategic roster construction, and a deep appreciation of the NFL.

---

## League Philosophy

We believe that sustained fantasy football success comes from:

* **Strategic Planning** — Year-over-year thinking, keeper valuation, and savvy draft preparation.
* **Statistical Analysis** — Grounding roster and lineup decisions in advanced metrics and game context.
* **Community Engagement** — Fostering camaraderie, active banter, and competitive integrity.
* **Continuous Evolution** — Adapting strategies and league governance to elevate the competition each season.

---

## League Overview & Format

* **Hosting Platform:** **Sleeper** ([AFFL League Home](https://sleeper.com/leagues/{{ site.current_league_id }}))
* **League Size:** **12 Teams** divided into two 6-team divisions (**Yin** and **Yang**)
* **Scoring Format:** **Full PPR** (1.0 Point Per Reception, 6 pt passing/rushing/receiving TDs, fractional yardage)
* **Starting Lineup (9 Starters):** QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX (W/R/T), K, DEF
* **Roster Depth:** 5 Bench spots + 1 IR / Reserve slot
* **Keepers:** **1 Keeper** per team (forfeiting the team's Round 1 draft pick; due 2 weeks before the draft)
* **Draft Type:** **Slow Snake Draft** hosted in the Sleeper Draft Room
* **Playoff Tournament:** **6 Teams, 3 Weeks** (Weeks 15–17); Top 2 division winners earn 1st-round byes; 6-team **Toilet Bowl** with the #1 draft pick incentive for next season's winner.
* **Trade Deadline:** Conclusion of **Week 13**

For complete constitution rules, tiebreakers, and governance policies, visit the [**Official Rules & Constitution**]({{ site.baseurl }}/rules/) page.

## Core Values

1. **Integrity & Sportsmanship** — Fair competition, good-faith trading, and zero tolerance for collusion.
2. **Active Competition** — Fielding competitive lineups every week from kickoff through championship week.
3. **Community & Fun** — Enjoying the thrill of game day together across league chat and matchup banter.
