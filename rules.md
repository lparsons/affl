# AFFL League Constitution & Custom Rules

These rules govern The Art of Fantasy Football League (AFFL) and provide structure that carries over across seasons and hosting platforms. In cases where they conflict with default hosting platform settings, these league rules override platform defaults.

---

## 1. Fair Transaction & Fair Trade Rule

This rule is designed to prevent a style of play that takes away from the fun and integrity of the game.

Transactions (trades and free agent pickups and claims) must be made with the intent of improving one's own team and not made in collusion with another manager. 

* **Prohibited Actions:** 
  - Giving up part-way through the season and trading top players for unequal value to benefit another team. *(Please do not give up part-way through the season; it spoils the competition for everyone).*
  - "Lending" or trading a player temporarily to an opponent of a division rival just to help give that rival a loss.
* **Permitted Actions:**
  - Managers **can** make an "unbalanced" trade, as long as it is done in good faith with the intent to directly improve their own team.

### Dispute & Voting Process
If you feel that a trade or transaction violates this rule:
1. **Submit an Objection:** Contact the league commissioner.
2. **League Review:** Reasonable objections (as determined by the commissioner) will be put to a **2-day league vote**.
3. **Reversal:** If more than **50% of the league** votes that the transaction was in violation of league rules, the transaction will be reversed.
4. **Scoring Adjustments:** If any player involved in an illegal transaction played after the trade but before the vote to reverse, those points will **not count** for any team that week.

> **Note:** Repeated or grievous violations of this rule may incur additional penalties up to and including expulsion from the league at the commissioner's discretion.

## 2. In-Season Transaction & Lineup Deadlines

* **Lineup Changes:** Managers may adjust starting lineups up until the **official kickoff time** of each player's individual game. Once a game begins, all participating players lock for the week.
* **Free Agent Add/Drops:** Unlocked free agents may be added or dropped at any time until their game begins. Dropped players or players whose games have started are placed on waivers.
* **Waiver Wire Claims:** Weekly waiver claims run automatically on the platform schedule (typically Wednesday mornings). Once waivers clear, all unclaimed players become standard free agents.

*Violation of fair transaction rules or exploiting platform glitches may result in penalties, up to and including transaction reversal and commissioner intervention.*

---

## 3. Trading & Draft Pick Exchanges

Trading can occur at any time, but must be in good faith. Teams may trade draft picks and/or keeper rights. *(Note: Selecting a keeper forfeits early draft picks as described in the Keeper Rules).*

### Off-Season Trading
Trading during the off-season (when automated platform trading is inactive) is moderated by the commissioner:
1. Once a trade is agreed upon, both managers must contact the commissioner with the terms.
2. The commissioner will verify fairness and manually update rosters and/or draft picks.
3. Once processed, league managers may dispute the trade under the standard **Fair Trade** dispute rules.

### Trade Deadline
* The trade deadline occurs at the conclusion of **Week 13** of the NFL regular season. No further in-season trades are permitted once Week 13 concludes until the off-season window opens.

---

## 4. Keeper Rules

Before the start of the next season, teams may designate **one (1) keeper**:
* The kept player remains on that team's roster for the upcoming season.
* **Draft Pick Cost:** Selecting a keeper forfeits that team's **Round 1 draft pick**.
* **Deadline:** Keeper selections must be finalized **two weeks before the start of the AFFL draft** (4 weeks prior to the NFL season start).

---

## 5. Draft Order Determination

Draft order for the next season is determined by playoff finishes for playoff teams, and regular season records for non-playoff teams.

### The Toilet Bowl Incentive
* The winner of the **Toilet Bowl** receives the **#1 overall pick in the first round after keeper selections**:
  - If the Toilet Bowl winner chooses **1 keeper**, they forfeit their 1st round pick but receive the **1st pick of the 2nd round (Pick 2.01)**. In Round 3 and subsequent rounds, they return to their standard draft position based on regular season record.
  - If the Toilet Bowl winner chooses **no keeper**, they receive the **#1 overall pick in the 1st round (Pick 1.01)**.

### Draft Order Allocation (12-Team League)
| Picks | Qualifying Teams | Tiebreaker / Order |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Picks 1 – 6** | Non-playoff teams | Reverse regular season record (worst record gets Pick 1) |
| **Picks 7 – 8** | Wild Card round losers | Worst regular season record gets Pick 7 |
| **Picks 9 – 10** | Conference Semifinal / Round 2 losers | Worst regular season record gets Pick 9 |
| **Pick 11** | Super Bowl Runner-up | — |
| **Pick 12** | Super Bowl Champion | — |

*Ties are broken by regular season record and standard division rank tiebreakers.*

## 6. Playoff Structure

### 12-Team League Playoff Format
#### Divisions:
* **2 Divisions:** **Yin** and **Yang** (6 teams each)

#### Qualifying Teams (6 Teams):
* **2 Division Champions** (Top team in Yin & top team in Yang) — Earn **Seeds 1 & 2** and receive a **First-Round Bye**.
* **4 Wildcard Teams** (Next 4 teams with the best records regardless of division) — Earn **Seeds 3 through 6**.

#### Playoff Schedule:
* **Week 15 — Wild Card Round:**
  - Game 1: Seed 3 vs. Seed 6
  - Game 2: Seed 4 vs. Seed 5
  - *(Seeds 1 and 2 on First-Round Bye)*
* **Week 16 — Semifinals / Conference Championships:**
  - Seed 1 vs. Lowest remaining seed from Wild Card round
  - Seed 2 vs. Highest remaining seed from Wild Card round
* **Week 17 — AFFL Super Bowl & Toilet Bowl Final:**
  - Semifinal winners compete for the **AFFL League Championship**
  - Consolation / Toilet Bowl final matchups conclude

---

### 10-Team League Playoff Format (Historical / Alternate)
Two-week playoff tournament (Weeks 15 & 16) where all teams continue competing across two brackets:

* **League Championship Bracket (Top 4 Teams):**
  - Two division champions + two wild cards.
  - Round 1 (Week 15): Seed 1 vs. Seed 4; Seed 2 vs. Seed 3.
  - Round 2 (Week 16): Winners play for League Championship; losers play consolation for 3rd place.
* **Toilet Bowl Bracket (Seeds 5 – 10):**
  - Week 15: Game 1-1 (5 vs 6), Game 1-2 (7 vs 8), Game 1-3 (9 vs 10).
  - Week 16: Winner of Game 1-1 vs Winner of Game 1-2 plays for **Toilet Bowl Championship**. Loser of 1-2 vs Loser of 1-3 plays the Sacko Bowl; the loser is crowned **"The Sacko"** and faces designated league punishment.

## 7. League Rule Changes & Governance

Proposals for rule changes should be submitted to the league and the commissioner. Any proposal **seconded by another league manager** is eligible for an official league vote.

### 1. General League Decisions
* Decisions on non-constitutional league matters (e.g. league expansion) are decided by a **simple majority (>50%)**.
* If three or more options exist, the top two advance to a runoff. In a multi-way tie, the commissioner selects the runoff options.

### 2. Off-Season Rule Changes
* **Window:** End of NFL Pro Bowl until **two weeks before the draft**.
* **Requirement:** Must be ratified by a **two-thirds (2/3) majority vote**. At least 50% of league managers must cast a ballot.

### 3. In-Season Rule Changes
* **Window:** Two weeks before the draft through the end of the Pro Bowl.
* Changes during the season are discouraged and require a **unanimous vote** of all league managers.
* In-season changes take effect **two weeks (8+ days) after ratification** to avoid disrupting active matchups.

## 9. Commissioner's Authority

In all cases, the commissioner retains the authority to veto or implement changes, transactions, or adjustments—regardless of vote outcomes—if deemed necessary for the health, integrity, and good of the league.
